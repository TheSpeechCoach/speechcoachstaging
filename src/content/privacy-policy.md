## 1. Introduction

The Speech Coach Limited ("The Speech Coach", "we", "us", "our") is committed to the highest standards of confidentiality, privacy, data protection, safeguarding, professional integrity, and information security.

This policy explains how personal data, recordings, communications, intellectual property, and confidential information are collected, processed, retained, protected, and managed in accordance with:

- the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR");
- the Data Protection Act 2018 ("DPA 2018");
- applicable safeguarding obligations under the Children Act 1989, the Children Act 2004, Working Together to Safeguard Children 2023, and associated statutory guidance;
- professional conduct standards set by the Health and Care Professions Council ("HCPC") and the Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists ("RCSLT"); and
- all other relevant UK legal requirements.

This policy applies to all clients, prospective clients, corporate clients, workshop attendees, training participants, website users, and any individual engaging with The Speech Coach Limited.

This policy constitutes our Privacy Notice for the purposes of Articles 13 and 14 UK GDPR and should be read alongside any engagement agreement or terms of service provided to you.

## 2. Professional Standards & Safeguarding

The Speech Coach provides two distinct categories of service, each governed by different professional frameworks:

### 2.1 Regulated Speech and Language Therapy

Where The Speech Coach provides regulated speech and language therapy services, those services are delivered in accordance with obligations under the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC) Standards of Conduct, Performance and Ethics and the Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists (RCSLT) professional guidelines. In this context, HCPC registration imposes binding professional, ethical, and confidentiality obligations.

### 2.2 Communication & Executive Coaching

Where The Speech Coach provides communication coaching, executive presentation coaching, or advisory services that do not constitute regulated clinical practice, those services are informed by the professional standards of the HCPC and RCSLT as a framework of best practice, but do not constitute regulated therapeutic intervention. Clients engaging for coaching purposes should note this distinction.

In all cases, The Speech Coach holds a current Enhanced DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) certificate, renewed in accordance with applicable safeguarding guidance, and applies rigorous safeguarding obligations across all engagements.

## 6. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data under one or more of the following lawful bases under Article 6 UK GDPR:

### 6.1 Contractual Necessity (Article 6(1)(b))

Processing necessary to deliver coaching, advisory, or regulated therapy services pursuant to our engagement.

### 6.2 Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f))

Processing carried out in pursuit of our legitimate interests in professional administration, service quality, communication analysis, safeguarding, and operational management, where such interests are not overridden by your interests or fundamental rights and freedoms. We have conducted Legitimate Interests Assessments (LIAs) in respect of processing carried out on this basis. A summary of those assessments is available on request by contacting info@thespeech.coach.

### 6.3 Consent (Article 6(1)(a))

Where specific consent is required — including in respect of testimonials, recordings used beyond standard coaching analysis, or special category data where Article 9(2)(a) applies — we will obtain your explicit written consent prior to such processing.

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting info@thespeech.coach. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

### 6.4 Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c))

Processing necessary to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations, including under tax, accounting, and safeguarding legislation.

### 6.5 Vital Interests / Public Task (Articles 6(1)(d) and 6(1)(e))

In limited circumstances, particularly in connection with safeguarding obligations, we may process personal data to protect the vital interests of a data subject or another person, or in connection with a task carried out in the public interest.

## 7. Confidentiality

All coaching sessions, therapy sessions, communications, recordings, materials, notes, and discussions are treated as strictly confidential.

Information will not be disclosed to third parties unless:

- we are legally required to do so;
- disclosure is operationally necessary and proportionate;
- safeguarding obligations require disclosure; or
- explicit prior written consent has been obtained from you.

This duty of confidentiality extends to commercially sensitive, personal, strategic, financial, investment-related, political, proprietary, or reputationally sensitive information shared during sessions.

### 7.1 Safeguarding Exceptions

Where we have reasonable grounds to believe that disclosure is necessary to prevent serious harm to you, to a child, or to a third party, we may be required — and in some circumstances legally obliged — to share information with appropriate statutory authorities, safeguarding bodies, or other relevant professionals, notwithstanding our general confidentiality obligations. Where possible and appropriate, we will inform you before making such a disclosure. Our safeguarding obligations take precedence over general confidentiality duties where a conflict arises.

In the context of regulated therapy with children and young persons, our obligations under Working Together to Safeguard Children 2023, the Children Act 1989, and the Children Act 2004 apply in full.

## 8. Session Recordings

### 8.1 Purpose of Recordings

Sessions may be audio and/or video recorded for the following purposes:

- Note-taking and session accuracy;
- Coaching and communication analysis;
- Behavioural observation and developmental review; and
- Preparation of follow-up recommendations.

Recordings are used exclusively within the coaching or therapy context for the benefit of the individual client.

### 8.2 Lawful Basis for Recording

Session recordings are processed on the basis of our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR) in providing accurate, high-quality services, or on the basis of contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b)), as applicable. Where recordings involve special category data, we will obtain your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)) or rely on another applicable Article 9 condition.

### 8.3 Notification of Recording

We will take reasonable steps to notify you before a session is recorded. This may include written notice in engagement communications, verbal notification at the outset of the session, or automatic notifications generated by video conferencing platforms (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet). You are always invited to raise any concerns about recording before or at the outset of a session, and we will seek to accommodate reasonable requests.

### 8.4 Confidentiality & Restricted Use of Recordings

Session recordings are treated as strictly confidential and securely stored. Recordings will not be publicly shared, distributed externally, sold, published, licensed, used for marketing, or uploaded into AI systems or datasets, without your explicit prior written consent.

Session recordings, coaching observations, frameworks, analyses, methodologies, and associated materials also constitute the intellectual property of The Speech Coach Limited (see Section 9).

### 8.5 Your Rights in Relation to Recordings

You may submit a Subject Access Request to obtain a copy of recordings containing your personal data (see Section 14). One standard digital copy will ordinarily be provided free of charge. Requests will be processed within one calendar month, subject to identity verification. Where requests are complex, this may be extended by up to two further months with notification to you.

The Speech Coach Limited reserves the right to redact or restrict portions of recordings where necessary to protect third-party confidentiality, legally privileged material, proprietary methodologies, or the rights of others, in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

### 8.6 Permitted Client Use of Recordings

Where recordings are provided to you, they are supplied solely for your personal review, communication analysis, and developmental purposes. Recordings may not be redistributed, commercially exploited, shared publicly, uploaded into AI systems (including Read AI, ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, or similar), or used for any external educational or commercial purpose without our explicit prior written permission.

### 8.7 Retention of Recordings

Session recordings are retained securely for 30 days from the date of the session and are then permanently deleted, unless a longer retention period has been expressly agreed in writing. Earlier deletion may be requested by contacting info@thespeech.coach and will be carried out promptly, subject to any overriding legal or safeguarding obligations.

The coaching and therapy of children and young persons (under 18 years of age) is a regular part of our practice. Where we work with minors:

- Parental or guardian consent will be obtained before processing the minor's personal data, save where applicable safeguarding legislation provides otherwise;
- Enhanced safeguarding obligations apply in full, including those under the Children Act 1989, the Children Act 2004, and Working Together to Safeguard Children 2023;

- Any safeguarding concerns arising in the course of an engagement will be handled in accordance with our safeguarding policy and applicable statutory obligations, which may require disclosure to statutory bodies without prior notification to the client.

The Speech Coach Limited will not knowingly process personal data of children under 13 years of age via its website without verifiable parental or guardian consent.
